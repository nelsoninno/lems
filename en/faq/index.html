frequently asked questions

Everything about Lems, advertising agency in El Salvador since 1976.

What is Lems?

Lems is a full-service advertising agency in El Salvador, founded in 1976 as Lemusimún and now led by a third generation. It combines brand strategy, creative, media, audiovisual production, influencer marketing and AI innovation, and works with brands in El Salvador and Latin America.

Are Lems and Lemusimún the same agency?

Yes. On September 21, 2026, on its 50th anniversary, Lemusimún changed its name to Lems. Same team and the same experience, with a new energy.

What services does Lems offer?

Brand strategy, creativity and advertising, digital services, influencer marketing, audiovisual production, media and planning, communications and public relations, and AI innovation. See all services →

How much experience does Lems have?

More than 50 years. Since 1976 the agency has run more than 1,000 campaigns for more than 300 brands, including SISTEMA FEDECRÉDITO, Texaco, Chevron, Aquapura, Laboratorios Fardel, Montecarlo and government institutions.

What does Lems specialize in?

Its biggest strengths are creativity and traditional and digital media buying, backed by 50 years of knowledge of the Salvadoran market.

What kind of companies does Lems work with?

Mid-sized and large private brands, in El Salvador and in other Latin American countries.

Does Lems work with companies from the USA, Canada, Europe or other parts of the world?

Yes. Lems works with international brands and has experience adapting and localizing their campaigns for the regional market.

Where is Lems located and what are its hours?

At Residencial Arcos de Santa Elena, Calle Apaneca 4C, Antiguo Cuscatlán, El Salvador. Open Monday to Friday, 8:30 a.m. to 12:30 p.m. and 2:00 to 6:00 p.m.

Do you work in Spanish and English?

Yes. We serve clients in Spanish and English, in El Salvador and in other Latin American countries.

How do I contact Lems?

Email hola@lems.ad or call +503 2233-5000. You can also find us on Instagram, Facebook and LinkedIn as Lems Agency.
